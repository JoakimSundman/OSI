## Main

The physical layer is the first layer of the OSI model and is responsible for the physical connection between devices. It defines the electrical, mechanical, and functional specifications for activating, maintaining, and deactivating the physical link in a network. The physical medium can be copper (eletrical), fiber (optical), WIFI (RF).
